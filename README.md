#Move Tunes
#Move Tunes is a fun, interactive music app that helps you connect with music through hand gestures. It currently features three classical instruments: piano, guitar, and violin.
#The app offers three modes, each designed for a different kind of musical experience:
#Teaching Mode
Choose a song from our selection and learn to play it step by step. Move Tunes tracks your accuracy as you practice, helping you see your progress and improve.
#Game Mode
Test your gesture speed in our note-drop game. Burst the falling notes to score points and enjoy a fast-paced musical challenge.
#Free Mode
Play whatever you like, with no restrictions. You can also record your own music and revisit your creations.
Whether you’re learning a song, chasing a high score, or making music your own, Move Tunes gives you a playful way to explore piano, guitar, and violin.
